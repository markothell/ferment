A circle, in Culture

Activities are threaded in the order they happened, spiraling in to the question that's open now at the center. Faint participation lines are always visible. Hovering an activity brings its own lines to full strength and shows what it was about. Use the History buttons to compare a young circle with only 3 or 4 activities against one that's ten months in.

The thread. Straight segments from the oldest activity on the outside to the open question in the middle. The nodes are spaced along an even spiral, so a longer history fills the space on its own. A young circle has a short spiral near the center, and an older one reaches the seats.

Faint participation lines. On by default, with every line at 10% strength. The middle gets a web-like texture without showing who did what.

Focus. Hovering, tabbing to, or tapping an activity brings its lines to full strength in its own color: saffron for explored, green for open now. Other activities and the waiting ideas ease back slightly. Seats never dim, and there's no extra outline.

Colors. Seats are Whey, explored activities are soft Saffron, one person's waiting ideas are Beet, and open now is Verdigris, still the only green. Initials and names are in Hanken Grotesk.

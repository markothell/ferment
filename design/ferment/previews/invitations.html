Someone suggests, a host says yes

Today any member can invite anyone, and anyone in the community can walk into any running circle. Both become asks. Any member suggests a new person, and a host confirms before any email goes out. A seat in a running circle, for someone new or someone already here, is the circle's to give, with the same support it gives a question.

    Shape

    Two asks, one pattern

    Into the community: a member suggests someone, and a host confirms. The suggestion carries a name, an email and an optional note. Nothing reaches the person until a host says yes. The email then still comes from the member who suggested them ("Kofi invited you to Tables"), because people join people. When a host suggests someone, it counts as confirmed and goes straight out.

    Into a circle: the circle supports the seat, as it supports a question. A seat request needs the same support as a new question in that circle: a third of its members, at least 2. The circle's host can make room at once, the same way they promote a question. The member who suggested someone counts as a supporter. A person asking for themselves doesn't, since they aren't in the circle yet.

    Both asks start together. When Kofi suggests Ana and names Bread, the host's confirmation and Bread's support run side by side. Ana is only invited into the circle in the email if Bread has already agreed. If Bread agrees later, she gets a "you're in Bread" notification when she arrives. If Bread never agrees, she never hears about the circle, so nobody is turned away where they can see it.

    Forming circles don't change. A proposal is already an open invitation, so I'm in stays one tap until the circle forms. After that, joining goes through a seat request.

    Lifecycle

    Four states for each ask

    WaitingnominatedwaitingThe host sees it in Waiting for you, and the circle sees it in Asking to join, with its supports. It lapses after 14 days.
    Yespending → acceptedadmittedThe invitation is sent and the seat is held. The person joins the circle when they arrive, or at once if they're already in the community.
    Not nowdeclineddeclinedThe suggester is told, with the host's note if they left one. The person suggested hears nothing.
    Withdrawn or lapsedwithdrawn · lapsedThe suggester can withdraw their ask while it waits. After 14 days the clock lapses it and tells the suggester.

    On the circle map, a seat request that's waiting is drawn as a dashed empty seat on the ring, the same Beet colour as a waiting suggestion's spur. When it's admitted it becomes a seat. People see a seat being made, the same way they see a question being put forward.

    Rules

    What each ask checks

    domain/invites.ts, domain/seats.ts

    • One live ask per person. There's at most one waiting or sent invitation per email per community, and one waiting seat request per person per circle. A second suggestion for the same person shows the first ("Sol already suggested Ana, 2 days ago") instead of creating another.
    • Room is checked twice, when the ask is made and again when it's granted: the community against maxMembers, the circle against 12. A circle that's full takes no new seat requests.
    • Who can suggest. Any community member can suggest someone new. Any member of a circle can suggest an existing community member for it. Anyone in the community can ask for a seat in a running circle for themselves.
    • Who grants. Any community host confirms community invitations. For circle seats, it's the support threshold, or the circle's host making room. A circle with no host can still admit someone by support.
    • A seat held for someone new is tied to their invitation. If the invitation is declined, withdrawn or lapses, the seat request goes with it.
    • What a newcomer to a running circle sees: everything the circle has shared, with the same reveal rules as anyone else. The request card tells members this before they support ("Ana will see what Bread has shared").
    • Leaving and coming back works like joining: someone who left asks for a seat again.

    Existing rows. Pending invitations stay pending, since they were already sent. An invitation with a circleId becomes a seat request that's already admitted, because under the old rule the person who sent it could seat someone.

    Domain. createInvite() becomes suggestInvite(), which a host's own suggestion runs straight through. New: confirmInvite() and declineInvite(). acceptInvite() joins the community, then seats the person in every circle where their seat was admitted. New domain/seats.ts: askForSeat(), toggleSeatSupport(), makeRoom(), declineSeat(), withdrawAsk(), reusing approvalsNeeded() from rounds.ts. joinCircle() refuses an active circle unless it's called from an admitted seat.

A circle opens a round to its community

When a round closes, anyone in the circle can propose opening it to the community. Everyone who answered gets a yes or no on their own answer. Only the yeses go in. It goes up once three people have said yes, and anyone can take their answer back out at any time.

    Shape

    How far it goes, and what goes in

    Level 1: the communityEveryone in Tables can see a harvest. It shows on the circle's page from outside, and under its question in the feed. This is all that's built for now.
    Level 2: outsideOther communities, later, when sharing between communities is designed. Anonymity can be looked at then. Nothing here assumes it.
    The unit: one closed roundOpening a round isn't offered until the round has closed, so everyone sees exactly what they'd share, and next to whose answers.

    What's in it: the question, and the round's result for the people who said yes: their stories, their dots on a slider or a 2×2, or their words. Names stay on. Voice is shared like any other answer: one yes or no, whatever the type, and the author decides.

    What isn't: anyone who didn't say yes. Their answer isn't hidden in the harvest, it was never copied into it. The harvest says "4 of 6 shared" without naming the other two.

    No replies, no reactions. A harvest is something to read and listen to. If it sparks something, people can adapt the question or start a circle around it, as they already can in the feed.

    Consent

    Support is consent

    Anyone in the circle proposes. Proposing counts as the proposer's own yes, if they answered. A member who didn't answer the round can still propose; they just have nothing to share.

    Each person who answered decides about their own answer. They see it exactly as it would appear, and choose Share mine or Keep mine in the circle. Saying nothing is no. Choosing "keep mine" only stops the reminders; nobody is told who kept theirs.

    Three yeses puts it up. It goes up the moment the third person says yes, and stays open to more yeses until the week ends. After that, the list of sharers is fixed: people can still take theirs out, but no one new joins.

    If it never reaches three, it lapses quietly at the end of the week. The circle can propose again later.

    One harvest per round. A second proposal on the same round shows the first one instead.

    Lifecycle

    Four states

    GatheringgatheringProposed, fewer than 3 yeses. Only the circle sees it, on the round page and in their inbox. The week starts now.
    Upup3 or more yeses. Tables can see it. Until the week ends, more people can say yes.
    DowndownWithdrawals took it below 3. It disappears from outside. The people still in it are told.
    LapsedlapsedThe week ended with fewer than 3. Nothing was ever shown outside the circle.

    A harvest lasts until its sharers take it down. Whether a community can choose to let harvests fade is on hold.

    Withdrawing

    Always individual

    • Anyone can take their own answer out, any time. From outside it's simply gone: no gap, no "withdrawn" note. The count drops ("3 of 6 shared").
    • Below three, the harvest comes down. The same rule as going up. The remaining sharers get a notification.
    • There's no circle-wide takedown. Each answer belongs to the person who gave it.
    • The original answer is never touched. Taking yours out of a harvest removes the copy. Inside the circle the round reads exactly as before.
    • Leaving the circle doesn't take anything out. Your yes stands until you withdraw it, which you can still do from your inbox or the harvest itself.
    • Closing your account takes you out of every harvest. Inside circles, your answers show as "account closed".
    • Questions stay, answers can go. The prompt, its adaptations and where it spread are the community's and stay. Lineage keeps its links when content goes: "adapted from a question in Bread", "asked by an account since closed".

    The rules from 08 PROTOCOLS §5, carried over. Publishing writes a copy; nothing on responses ever becomes "public". The read side (domain/harvests.read.ts) imports only harvests and harvestItems, so it can't reach a private answer even by mistake. Leaving something out means it's never copied in. Withdrawal deletes the item row, not a flag on it.

    Voice. A shared voice answer points at the same recording as the original. The audio route serves it to anyone in the community only through a live harvest_items row. Taking it out removes that row, so the link stops working outside the circle while the original plays on inside.

    Open

    Still to decide

    Can someone change "keep mine" to "share mine" during the week?

    Proposed: yes. Until the week ends, either choice can be changed.

    After a lapse, how soon can the round be proposed again?

    Proposed: any time, but the proposer sees that it lapsed before ("Last tried 3 weeks ago, 2 said yes").

    Does a harvest count toward the feed's ranking?

    Proposed: no. The feed ranks by how many circles took a question up; a harvest shows alongside but doesn't move it.

    The "account closed" wording inside circles.

    Proposed: "account closed", in the faint ink, where the name was.

    Open questions to the whole community are already visible to everyone, so they don't need harvests. At level 2 they might.
